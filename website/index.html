A workshop for your job search.

Applysmith finds postings, scores them against you, tailors your résumé and fills the forms. It runs on your own Mac, and it never submits without you.

See how it works Try the demo first

For macOS 13 or later, with Google Chrome. Open source under the MIT licence.

1

It finds the postings.

Job sources, hiring signals and leads land in one inbox. Scans can run on a schedule, and a schedule can never fill a form or submit.

2

It weighs each one against you.

Every posting gets a score, and a report that names the deciding factors. A clear “skip” saves you the hour as surely as a match earns it.

3

It prepares the documents.

A résumé and a cover letter tailored to the posting, grounded in what you really did. Nothing is claimed that your own record does not support.

4

It fills the form. You sign it off.

Applications are filled in a Chrome window of its own, from answers you reviewed. A dry run fills every page and stops before Submit.

Yours, on your machine.

Nothing leaves the computer except calls to the services you choose to connect.

One server, on loopback only
A single process serves the dashboard, the scheduler and the agent. It listens to this computer and nothing else.
One folder you choose
Your profile, CV, answer bank and reports are plain files. Open them, edit them, back them up.
Secrets in the keychain
Passwords and API keys go to the macOS keychain, never into a file.

Rules it cannot break.

The product enforces them, so you do not have to remember them.

  • Submit is yours. Submitting needs your explicit request for that application, or a confirmation you complete in the dashboard.
  • No invented answers. A work-authorization or self-identification question without your answer is left blank.
  • No duplicates. A posting you apply to twice is stopped.
  • Schedules never submit. They can scan, read mail and send digests. They cannot fill a form or create an account.

Questions.

Will it ever submit an application without me?

No. A dry run fills every page and stops before Submit. Submitting needs your explicit request for that application, or a confirmation you complete in the dashboard. A schedule can never fill a form or submit.

Where does my data go?

It stays in one folder you choose. The only network traffic is to the services you connect: a language-model provider, Gmail, Telegram, job boards and the sites you apply to.

Do I need an AI account?

To evaluate postings, write documents and answer free-text questions, yes: one language-model provider on your own account. That can be an API key, your ChatGPT plan, or a command-line tool you are already signed in to. To look around, the Offline choice needs none, and its reports say so.

What does it cost?

Applysmith is free and open source under the MIT licence. What you pay for is your own model provider, held by a spend cap for each day: 2 US dollars unless you change it.

Does it run on Windows or Linux?

It supports macOS 13 or later with Google Chrome 120 or later. Other operating systems are unverified.
