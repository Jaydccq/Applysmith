# Narrative

This is the story of the fictional example persona, Alex Example. Cover
letters and free-text answers in the demo draw on it.

## What I am looking for

A senior platform or backend role on a team that treats its internal platform
as a product. Remote or hybrid near Durham, North Carolina, with at most two
office days a week.

## Proof points

- At Northwind Labs, forty teams moved to a self-service deployment pipeline
  and the median lead time fell from two days to forty minutes.
- At Contoso Cloud, an idempotent payment retry ended duplicate charges on 2
  million orders a month.
- A 23 percent cut in the platform's cloud bill without slowing anyone down.

## Things to avoid

- Claims about management experience: Alex has mentored, not managed.
- Buzzwords in place of numbers.
