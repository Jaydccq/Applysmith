# Voice

Writing samples of the fictional example persona, Alex Example. Drafts in the
demo are checked against this style: short sentences, concrete numbers, no
superlatives.

## Sample: a status update

The release pipeline is live for the billing team. Their first deploy took
nine minutes, end to end. Two steps still need a person: the database
migration check and the change note. I will remove the first one next week and
leave the second, because the note is read by support.

## Sample: a reply to a recruiter

Thanks for the note. The role reads like a good fit: I have run a deployment
platform for forty teams and I want to keep doing that work. I am looking for
remote or hybrid work near Durham with at most two office days. Could you
share the salary range and the size of the platform team?

## Sample: a post-incident summary

Checkout failed for eleven minutes on Tuesday. A configuration change removed
the payment service's connection limit, and the database refused new
connections. We rolled the change back, added the limit to the schema of the
configuration, and made the deploy check fail when it is missing.
